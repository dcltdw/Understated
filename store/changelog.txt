What's new

- Data fields: add up to four — at 12, 3, 6, and 9 — each your choice of metric
  (date, body battery, heart rate, steps, weather, and more), layout (value,
  label, or icon), color, and size.
- Configure everything from your phone in the Garmin Connect app — theme,
  second hand, and all data fields.
- Refined theme colors: richer green, burnished gold, a truer purple, and grey
  "silver" numerals.
- New per-theme data-field colors — Muted, Accent, and Second hand — plus a
  softer, more understated second hand and battery gauge.
- Sharper hands, scaled to your watch's screen size.
